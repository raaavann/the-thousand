# Inspiration Scan — imdb-top-1000-films

Quick scan for mechanisms to borrow, not surfaces to copy. Kept to what materially shaped the three option directions.

## Historical star atlases / constellation charts
**Borrow:** quiet dark ground, small always-hidden labels that appear only near the point of focus, connecting lines drawn only between genuinely related points (not decorative).
**Avoid:** implying real astronomical/scientific precision — our "constellations" are genre clusters, not measured positions; must not read as a scientific instrument.
**Applied to:** Option 1, The Constellation.

## Jacquard loom pattern cards / textile weave notation
**Borrow:** warp/weft as a literal structural grid where a single thread can carry meaning across multiple crossing points — a natural, non-metaphorical way to show one film belonging to two genres at once (it sits at the actual crossing).
**Avoid:** decorative "fabric texture" styling that has no data behind it — the weave must be a real structural placement rule, not a background pattern.
**Applied to:** Option 2, Genre Weave.

## Vintage trade-paper front pages (Variety, Hollywood Reporter, 1930s-50s)
**Borrow:** irregular column hierarchy, a dominant lead story next to small marginal items, headline weight doing what a summary sentence would otherwise do. Thematically apt (movie-trade press).
**Avoid:** real trade papers are text-dense; ours must stay image-led with the minimal copy budget already set by Narrator.
**Applied to:** Option 3, Genre Broadsheet.

## What none of the three should carry over
No dashboard filter rail, no KPI strip, no legend key floating in a corner, no scientific-instrument precision that the data doesn't actually have (especially for Option 1 — force-layout position is illustrative clustering, not a measured coordinate).
