# Product Brief — IMDB Top 1000 Films

## Audience
General film enthusiasts browsing a curated "best of" collection — not analysts. People who want to lose time in a beautiful archive of great films, not query a database.

## Purpose
A data-art-led, collection-style exploration of the IMDB Top 1000 films dataset. Discovery and appreciation, not comparison or argument-making.

## Narrative Posture
**Data-art-led explorer.** The visual composition (posters, ratings, genre, era) speaks first. Visible narration is minimal — orientation cues and caveats only, no argumentative copy.

## Screen Feel
**Studio/gallery.** The first screen should feel like walking into a curated exhibition of film posters — minimal chrome, no dashboard controls dominating the frame. Light utility (filter/sort) may exist but must stay quiet and secondary, never the dominant visual language.

## Aesthetic Family
**Editorial film-poster wall.** Warm paper/print tones, magazine-like grid, typographic accents — reads like a curated print zine, not an app.

## Color Bias
**Muted ink/charcoal only.** No dominant accent hue — posters themselves supply all color. UI chrome (type, rules, controls) stays monochrome/neutral so it never competes with the artwork.

## Anti-References (default to avoid)
- Generic analytics dashboard with cards, KPI tiles, and sidebar filters
- Bootstrap/Material "admin panel" look
- Bright saturated UI accent color competing with poster art
- Dense data-table-first presentation
- Search/filter chrome as the dominant first-screen element

## Design Principles
- Composition carries discovery — the poster grid/wall IS the interface, not a decoration next to it
- Typography does the only "loud" non-image work (editorial mastheads, pull quotes for taglines/overviews)
- Ratings, genre, era expressed visually (size, position, grouping) before they're expressed in text/numbers
- Any filtering/sorting must feel like flipping through a curated collection, not querying a database

## Accessibility Goals
- Poster alt text from Series_Title at minimum
- Sufficient contrast for any overlaid type on poster imagery (scrim/gradient as needed)
- Keyboard-navigable if interactive filtering is included
- Motion (if any) respects prefers-reduced-motion

## Intent Brief
Turn the IMDB Top 1000 dataset into a browsable, studio-gallery-feeling wall of film posters where genre, rating, era, and other attributes are legible through composition and typographic craft rather than dashboard chrome.
