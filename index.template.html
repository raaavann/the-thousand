Genres are anchored by how often they share a film relative to how common each one is, so Action sits beside Adventure and Biography beside History. Each film sits between its own genres, pulled hardest by its rarest. Position is affinity, not measurement.

The data

1,000 films from the IMDb Top 1000, 1920–2020. Every title rates between 7.6 and 9.3, so this is a narrow, already-selected band of acclaim, not the whole of cinema.

What it leaves out

Heavily skewed to English-language and post-1990 releases, and to films with enough votes to be ranked at all. Metascore is missing for 157 titles and box-office for 169; those slots stay blank rather than guessed.

Getting around

Hover a star to preview, click for the full record. Hover a genre to light its films. ← → move through the collection, Esc closes, / jumps to search.

Checking it

The layout hands over its own coordinates. Call __spatialAudit() in the console. Around 69% of films sit nearest a genre they actually belong to, against 12% for random placement. The figure moves a little with the shape of the frame.
